Good to know

Questions, answered.

Have a question that isn’t covered here? Tell us what you need help with.

Ask our team →
What kind of businesses do you work with?

We support startups, owner-managed businesses, professional firms and growing companies across a range of sectors. We’ll start by understanding your needs and confirming whether we’re the right fit.

Can you handle more than one service for my business?

Yes. We can discuss connected support across accounting, tax, GST, audit, payroll and company compliance, with a scope agreed around your requirements.

Do you work with businesses outside your city?

Many finance and compliance workflows can be handled remotely. Share your location and requirements and we’ll confirm the best way to work together.

What should I bring to an introductory conversation?

A short overview of your business, its structure and the questions on your mind is a good start. If you have recent filings or accounts, those can help too.

How do fees and engagement work?

After understanding the scope and timelines, we’ll explain the proposed work and fees before anything begins. Engagement terms are confirmed in writing.
